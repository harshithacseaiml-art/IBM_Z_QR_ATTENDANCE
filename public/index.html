QR Code Attendance System

A lightweight tool to register participants, generate a unique QR code per person, and mark attendance instantly at any workshop, hackathon session, or talk — replacing manual sign-in sheets for the 500+ attendees expected at IBM Z Summit 2026.

How it works

StepActionWhere
1Register a participant — system generates their unique QR codeAdmin
2Create a session (e.g. "Hackathon Day 1", "Keynote")Admin
3Volunteer scans each participant's QR at entryScan
4Organizers view live attendance counts and export CSVDashboard

Why this problem

With registrations, attendance, and communication currently handled manually, attendance tracking is one of the most repetitive and error-prone tasks at an event this size — and one of the easiest to solve well with a small, focused tool.
